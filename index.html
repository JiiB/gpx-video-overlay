Position the video where you like with its own controls (pause on a frame you recognize), then drag this slider through the GPX file until the badge above matches that frame, then hit the button.

3. Auto-calibrate from known power (optional)

The anchor above gets you roughly in sync, but small clock drift between the video and the GPX file can still leave things off by a second or two. If you filmed your cycling computer's screen at a few moments during the ride, you can use those as ground truth: enter the video timestamp and the power value you can read on screen at that moment for a couple of readings, then hit Auto-calibrate — it searches nearby offsets for the one that best matches the power the GPX file reports at those times.

Example: at 0:50 into the video your bike computer shows 369W, and at 2:58 it shows 384W. Enter those two rows and calibrate — this is entirely optional, skip it if you don't have footage of your computer or the anchor already looks accurate.
